Under the hood

Six signal families, all shown to you.

The engine mirrors the feature families used in the related literature, so every number in a report can be traced back to a rule you can read.

01 · Lexical

Sensationalism

Density of clickbait vocabulary drawn from Filipino, Taglish and English social posts — grabe, bistado, pasabog, shocking.

02 · Structural

Source attribution

Presence of named agencies, attributed statements, quotations and links. Unnamed sources are the strongest single indicator of unverified content.

03 · Affective

Emotional intensity

Exclamation density, outrage vocabulary and sentiment spikes that push readers to share before thinking.

04 · Visual

Formatting red flags

ALL-CAPS ratio, repeated punctuation, emoji stacking and shortened-post patterns typical of engagement bait.

05 · Linguistic

Hedging language

Filipino evidential markers — daw, umano, kuno — and English hedges like reportedly and allegedly.

06 · Forensic

Metadata & provenance

Container bytes, EXIF and C2PA blocks, editor signatures, generator fingerprints, geometry and bitrate profiles for media.
