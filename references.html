Sources

Everything this prototype leans on.

The literature reviewed by the study, reproduced from its reference list, plus the public fact-checking resources linked from every result.

01

LocalSabado, A. C. B., Sabado, V. M. B., Policarpio, R. J. E., Sueno, H. T., & Espiritu, J. M. R. C. (2024). Classifying fake news in Filipino language using improved DistilBERT model.

researchgate.net/publication/386401849

Attributed in the study's discussion as Carig & Manuel (2024); reported peak accuracy 94.7%.

02

LocalLaureta, M. P., Atienza, J. M. M., & Tapel, J. L. B. (2025). Deepfake speech detection: Identifying AI-generated and real human voices using hybrid convolutional neural network and long short-term memory model.

ejournals.ph/article.php?id=31036

CNN-LSTM pipeline over Mel spectrograms; reported accuracy 98.65%.

03

LocalJamen, R. J. B., & Reyes, R. C. (2023). Fake news detection in Philippine news corpus using LDA and sentiment analysis with machine learning.

proceedings.spp-online.org/article/view/SPP-2023-PB-40

TF-IDF and VADER sentiment features with LDA reduction; reported accuracy 94%.

04

LocalDemon, A., & Santos, V. (2025). Deepfake: A study on knowledge of media practitioners in Cotabato Province, Philippines.

cahaya-ic.com/index.php/JSKE/article/view/1798

Descriptive-correlational study of 25 radio practitioners; conceptual knowledge high, detection proficiency low.

05

InternationalChandraparsad, C., Kyaw, K. T., Jain, R., & Ayele, T. B. (2026). A systematic evaluation of transformer-based architectures for fake news detection in the low-resource Burmese language.

nature.com/articles/s41598-026-56647-7

mBERT, XLM-R, Burmese RoBERTa and XLM-R + CNN on 10,000 headlines; 97.3%–97.9% accuracy.

06

InternationalLekshmiAmmal, H. R., & Madasamy, A. K. (2025). A reasoning based explainable multimodal fake news detection for low resource language using large language models and transformers.

link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40537-025-01093-x

Tamil multimodal corpus; mBERT/XLM-R text plus ViT/DeiT vision features; macro F1 0.8736 with LIME explanations.

07

InternationalKumar, V., Raychaudhuri, R., Kundu, S., Mondal, A. K., Bhar, A., Bhattacharyya, S. K., & Biswas, N. (2026). An AI-powered fake news detection system: Analyzing misinformation in digital media.

link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-032-17184-9_30

Text, sentiment and social-context features compared across logistic regression, random forest and SVM.

08

InternationalMoyo, B. V. C., Tuyikeze, T., Matsebula, F., & Obagbuwa, I. C. (2026). An AI-driven conceptual framework for detecting fake news and deepfake content: A systematic review.

pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12989605

PRISMA review of 34 studies; three-tier framework binding detection outcomes, XAI metrics and legislative monitoring.
